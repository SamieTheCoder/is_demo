Frequently Ask Questions

Clear answers to help you understand our online schooling experience, accreditation, and student journey.

01 Is the school accredited internationally?

Yes, International Schooling is accredited by the NEASC, WASC, and Cognia.

02 Is the curriculum approved in the UAE?

Yes, International Schooling offers the American Curriculum, and it is globally approved.

03 Will the diploma be accepted by universities in the UAE and worldwide?

Yes, the American Diploma is accepted in the UAE and worldwide.

04 Is the school KHDA-approved or recognized?

No, International Schooling is not KHDA-approved or recognized.

05 Which curriculum does the school follow?

International Schooling follows the American Curriculum.

06 Do you offer American, British, IB, or another curriculum?

International Schooling offers the American Curriculum.

07 Are AP courses or advanced academic programs available?

Yes, AP courses or Advanced Academic programs are available.

08 How do you ensure academic quality and student performance?

International Schooling ensures student performance and academic quality through constant tests, quizzes, accredited standards, certified teachers, and continuous academic monitoring.

09 How are exams and assessments conducted?

All exams and assessments are conducted through online mode, under constant monitoring from teachers.

10 Can students transfer credits from previous schools?

Yes, students can transfer credits from previous schools.

11 Are classes live, recorded, or both?

All the classes are live.

12 Can my child study from the UAE while traveling internationally?

Yes, your child can study while travelling.

13 Is the learning schedule flexible for UAE students?

Yes, students can opt for a Learning schedule based on their availability.

14 How many live classes are conducted each week?

Depends on the selected learning program.

15 What technology or devices are required for online learning?

A computer/tablet with internet access.

16 Are teachers internationally certified and experienced?

Yes, all our teachers are internationally certified and experienced.

17 How does the school offer academic and counseling support?

International schooling offers academic, career, and personal counselling sessions throughout the academic year.

18 How do students interact with teachers and classmates?

Students interact with teachers and classmates through live classes and by participating in different extracurricular activities.

19 Can students apply to universities in the UAE, USA, Canada, and Europe?

International Schooling offers regular qualifications, including American High School Diploma and American transcripts, which are globally accepted for college and university applications, including the UAE, USA, Canada, and Europe.

20 Does the school help students prepare for college admissions and future careers?

Yes, International Schooling offers academic counselling for college admissions and future careers.
